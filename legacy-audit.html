Why inclination, and why not seismic

Node firmware

example

Reads an MPU-6050 at 100 Hz for two seconds, derives inclination from the gravity vector, reports the median inclination and the RMS dynamic acceleration once per interval. Bursting then reducing on-device is the part that matters: posting one instantaneous sample every fifteen seconds would be meaningless. The sketch also fires a local buzzer directly off the node's own anomaly check, before it ever tries to reach ThingSpeak \u2014 most IoT disaster-monitoring systems are documented as depending on a live cloud round trip for any alert at all, which is exactly the moment a storm is most likely to have taken the network down23.

    Rescue \u2014 AcuSearch

    EXPERIMENTAL PROPOSED

    The survivor-location half of this project, kept in the same file and held to the same rule: nothing here claims to detect anyone. What follows is the actual, narrow, evidence-backed gap this component targets, and a field tool for closing it.

    The honest finding, in one paragraph. Every proven victim-location method \u2014 UWB radar, seismic listening devices, the RECCO passive reflector, a 2026 phone-microphone method \u2014 is validated in structural rubble, snow, or open ground. None is validated in water-saturated clay and mud, the actual burial medium of a monsoon-triggered landslide. Published soil-radar depth guides show wet clay cuts penetration to roughly a third of dry sand's19, and a prior UWB/WiFi ranging trial recovered no UWB packets past 9 m even in drier rubble17. Landslide burial is also unusually lethal fast: about 90% of fatalities in one documented storm-triggered event were asphyxiation regardless of indoor/outdoor location20 \u2014 far less void space than an earthquake-collapsed building leaves. No public study answers whether a phone-based signal survives that medium at all. That is a measurement gap, not a missing invention, and the honest next step is to measure it before building a detector.

    What was rejected, and why

    Field tool \u2014 soil-pit link-budget log

    EXPERIMENTAL

    Bury a phone (Wi-Fi/BLE beacon or a fixed audible tone) or a wired contact microphone in local soil at a known depth and moisture level. Walk a receiver outward and log whether the signal is still detectable at each distance. This is the minimum experiment that can validate or kill any phone-based rescue concept for this deployment \u2014 log real results here rather than assuming an answer.

    What is not built, and will not appear connected

    PROPOSED
    • No detector. Nothing on this screen claims to locate a person. It logs an experiment and shows a reference checklist.
    • No radar, no WiFi-ranging engine, no acoustic-azimuth engine. Building one before the measurement above is answered would be building on an unverified premise \u2014 exactly the mistake this project already corrected once for HIMGAURAV's rainfall model.
    • No integration with SACHET, cell broadcast, or any government alert rail. That is a policy decision, not a software feature.
