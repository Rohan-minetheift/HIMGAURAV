    What the state labels mean
    StateMeaning in this prototype
    NO DATAUsable rainfall data is missing or stale. This is not a safe state.
    NORMALThe model-grid rainfall series stays below the published intensity–duration threshold at every tested duration.
    MONITORThe model-grid rainfall series crosses the threshold, but not strongly and 30-day antecedent modelled rainfall is below 110 mm.
    WATCHThe model-grid threshold is strongly exceeded, or crossing occurs with ≥110 mm antecedent 30-day modelled rainfall.
    WARNINGWATCH model-grid rainfall state plus an abnormal bound ground sensor. This remains a prototype operator state, not an official public warning.
    
        
    Data labels — what is live and what is not
    LIVE fetched during this browser session. DERIVED calculated locally from stated inputs. REFERENCE static dated fact. SENSOR field feed. SIMULATED demo-only. EXPERIMENTAL method not validated for operational warning.
    
        
    Rainfall screening formula
    Intensity–duration threshold used: I = 7.20 · D−0.26, with D in days and I in mm/day. Durations tested: 1, 2, 3, 5, 7 and 10 days. A 30-day antecedent total of 110 mm is used as an additional watch condition. The threshold was derived for Shimla; use elsewhere is explicitly an extrapolation.
    
        
    Ground-sensor method
    The browser reads a slow tilt/inclination series. Baseline is the recent median; noise is estimated with median absolute deviation (MAD), scaled by 1.4826. An anomaly requires three recent samples all farther than three noise units from the baseline. This is intentionally different from pretending that 15-second cloud samples are a seismic waveform.
    
        
    Limitations you should not miss
    • This is a research / student prototype, not an operational life-safety system.
    • Open-Meteo values are numerical weather-model grid values, not local rain-gauge observations.
    • Most rainfall threshold exceedances do not become landslides.
    • The published threshold used by the Himachal prototype was derived for Shimla. It is not used to generate a Northeast LIVE risk state.
    • Northeast DEMO values are deterministic scripted presentation data and are visually separated from LIVE mode.
    • ThingSpeak and a browser are not a resilient emergency communications path.
    • The site map and road graph are interface visualisations, not authoritative GIS layers.
    • Official decisions must come from the responsible government / disaster-management authorities.
